Every on-call engineer understands this situation well. An alert fires at 2 a.m., engineers spend the first five minutes figuring out what it means, the next few minutes searching Confluence for the relevant runbook, and finally start doing something useful. By that point, an automated system that could have classified the alert and retrieved the right procedure, proposed a remediation plan, and opened a ticket in thirty seconds has saved you nothing because it didn’t exist.
