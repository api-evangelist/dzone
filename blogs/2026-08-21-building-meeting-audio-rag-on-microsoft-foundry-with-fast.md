Every recorded meeting your organization has ever held is already a knowledge base. It just happens to be stored in the least queryable format imaginable, which is a wall of MP4 files sitting in a storage account that nobody opens twice. The good news is that the gap between that wall of files and a working question-answering agent is now much shorter than it used to be, because Microsoft Foundry ships the two halves you need in one place.
