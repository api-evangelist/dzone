The modern enterprise generates and consumes unprecedented volumes of data across operational systems, customer interactions, partner ecosystems, cloud applications, IoT devices, and AI platforms. At the same time, AI systems are becoming major consumers of enterprise data, making decisions, generating content, recommending actions, and automating workflows. Poor data quality is no longer just a reporting issue; it is also an AI issue.
