The evolution from monolithic applications to microservices transformed enterprise software by decomposing business capabilities into independently deployable services. REST APIs, asynchronous messaging, and service discovery enabled systems that scaled both organizationally and technically. Although this model remains effective for deterministic business logic, the emergence of AI agents introduces a different execution paradigm.
