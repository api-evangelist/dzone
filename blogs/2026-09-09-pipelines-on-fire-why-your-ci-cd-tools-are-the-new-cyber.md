Fifteen years in, and the conversation I have most often with security leads still starts the same way: how's your perimeter, how's your endpoint coverage, how's your SOC staffed? Almost nobody opens with "how's your pipeline." That's the gap I want to talk about, because 2025 was the year the gap turned into a crater. Here's the number that should reorder every security roadmap for 2026: major DevOps platforms — GitHub, GitLab, Azure DevOps, and Atlassian's Jira and Bitbucket — patched 236 vulnerabilities in 2025, according to GitProtect's DevOps Threats Unwrapped report.
