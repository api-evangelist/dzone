Batch processing remains vital because many business operations aren't suited to interactive requests. Tasks such as recalculating prices, reconciling transactions, migrating records, generating reports, processing invoices, reclassifying customers, or applying rules across millions of records may require considerable time. Handling these as standard requests leads to fragile systems, increased user wait times, frequent timeouts, challenging retries, and possible data inconsistencies.
