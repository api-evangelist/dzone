Al has blindsided cloud native infrastructure management, rendering established platform engineering woefully inadequate. Original platform engineering often suffers from a developer-only focus, but a platform that serves only one persona in a multi-persona organization ultimately delivers a shrinking fraction of its potential enterprise value. Autonomous agents are already beginning to write, review, test, and deploy code or fixes with or without human intervention.
