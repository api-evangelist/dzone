A humanoid robot you can build with a desktop 3D printer is lowering the barrier to experimenting with machines that usually cost far more. Researchers at the University of California, Berkeley, have developed the Berkeley Humanoid Lite, an open-source humanoid robot designed to give students, hobbyists and researchers a cheaper way to experiment with robotics.
