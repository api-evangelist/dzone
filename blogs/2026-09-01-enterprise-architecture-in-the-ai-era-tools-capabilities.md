An enterprise architecture (EA) tool is a software platform that enterprises use to capture, connect, and continuously maintain a structured picture of the enterprise covering strategies, business capabilities, processes, applications, data, technologies, and the relationships between all these elements. An EA tool acts as a Central Enterprise Repository (a “single source of truth”) that architects and other stakeholders use to model both the current state of the enterprise and the desired future state.
