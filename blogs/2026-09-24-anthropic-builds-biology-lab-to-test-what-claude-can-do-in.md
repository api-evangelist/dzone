Anthropic has spent years warning about the risks of increasingly capable AI. Now it is testing what those systems can do inside a real biology lab. The Claude maker has established a wet lab in the San Francisco Bay Area where it can conduct physical biological experiments, expanding its life sciences work beyond computer-based research.
