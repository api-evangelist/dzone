Some of Silicon Valley’s fiercest AI rivals are finding rare common ground: frontier development may need to slow down. Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei called on developers to slow the pace of increasing the capabilities of advanced AI models, warning that faster progress could outstrip existing safeguards. OpenAI CEO Sam Altman and xAI leader Elon Musk publicly endorsed the general direction of his argument.
