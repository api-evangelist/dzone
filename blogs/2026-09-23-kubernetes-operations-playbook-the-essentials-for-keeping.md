Kubernetes environments can drift, accumulate one-off fixes, and diverge across teams until a routine deploy breaks or a cost spike forces a review. This checklist gives platform, SRE, and engineering teams a way to keep clusters, deployments, and automation manageable as Kubernetes operations scale and teams grow.
