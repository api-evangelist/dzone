Quick Summary Both Embabel and LangGraph4j let a Java developer build multi-step AI agents without leaving the JVM. Embabel hands the framework a goal and a bag of typed actions, and lets a planner decide the order on its own. LangGraph4j asks the developer to draw the exact graph of nodes and edges by hand.
