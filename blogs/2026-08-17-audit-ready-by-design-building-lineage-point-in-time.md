Compliance Checkbox vs. Architectural Constraint Most data platforms treat audit-readiness as a downstream concern. The pipelines are built, the warehouse is populated, the dashboards ship, and only then does someone ask how the platform would respond to a regulator's request to reconstruct account balances as of a date eighteen months ago, or to prove that a reported figure hasn't been altered since submission.
