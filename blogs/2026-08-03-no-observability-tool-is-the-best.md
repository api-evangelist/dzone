Recently , I made a comment about the idea of there being a “best” monitoring tool: In fact, let’s get this out in the open: There simply isn’t a singular “best” monitoring tool out there any more than there’s one singular “best” programming language, or car model, or pizza style.* There isn’t a single tool which will cover 100% of your needs in every single use case.
