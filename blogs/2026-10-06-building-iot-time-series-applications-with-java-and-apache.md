Apache IoTDB is well-suited for environments where time-series data from connected devices and industrial systems is generated continuously and requires efficient querying. Typical use cases include monitoring temperature, pressure, vibration, energy usage, machine status, and device telemetry. This approach also applies to manufacturing, smart infrastructure, fleet monitoring, utilities, and edge computing.
