A detector I built was scoring 0.067 recall on temporal errors, meaning it caught about one in fifteen of the wrong dates it was supposed to find. Wrong dates are supposed to be the easy category: extract the years from the claim, extract the years from the source, compare. There is no semantics to get wrong.
