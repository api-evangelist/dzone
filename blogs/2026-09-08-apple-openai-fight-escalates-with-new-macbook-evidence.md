Apple is turning a former engineer’s old MacBook into the latest flashpoint in its increasingly bitter fight with OpenAI. Apple told a federal court Monday that evidence recovered from former engineer Chang Liu’s Apple-issued MacBook strengthens its claims that confidential company technology was accessed and used after he joined OpenAI.
