Meta has spent years helping businesses advertise on Facebook and Instagram and talk to customers through WhatsApp. Now it wants its AI working more deeply within those businesses. The company launched Meta Enterprise Platform on Monday, a new business focused on turning its AI models, agents, coding tools, and infrastructure into products companies can deploy themselves.
