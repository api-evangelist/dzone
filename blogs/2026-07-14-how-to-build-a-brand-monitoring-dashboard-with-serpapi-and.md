Knowing what people say about your product usually means checking Google News, scrolling through YouTube, and digging into different social media threads. That's three tabs, three interfaces, and no way to compare what you find. This tutorial builds a single dashboard that pulls brand mentions from all three sources using Python and SerpApi .
