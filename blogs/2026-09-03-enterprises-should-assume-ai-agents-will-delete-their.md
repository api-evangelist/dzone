Traditional identity mechanisms weren’t built to account for the autonomous and unpredictable nature of AI agents. So enterprises shouldn’t be surprised when they make unpredictable – or even destructive – decisions, like deleting a company’s entire production base. Without the right guardrails in place, these new actors will continue to wreak havoc across organizations.
