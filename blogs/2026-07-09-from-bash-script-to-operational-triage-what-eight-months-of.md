In November 2025, I published a Bash script that analyzed Kubernetes clusters in about 60 seconds. It generated HTML reports, surfaced crash loops, orphaned resources, and other operational issues that were easy to overlook. The most interesting part wasn't the script — it was what happened after people started running it.
