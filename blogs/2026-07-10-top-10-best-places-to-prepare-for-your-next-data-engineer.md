Landing a data engineering role means clearing a gauntlet that no other software discipline has to face all at once: airtight SQL, production-grade Python, data modeling instincts, distributed-compute fluency (Spark, warehouses, ETL), and system design that has to survive real data volume. Generic coding prep barely scratches the surface, and "just grind LeetCode" advice falls apart the moment an interviewer asks you to model a slowly changing dimension or reason about a skewed join. So we did the work.
