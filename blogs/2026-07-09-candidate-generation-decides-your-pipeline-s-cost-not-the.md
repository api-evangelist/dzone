When the Most Capable Model Is the Wrong Starting Point The fastest way to exceed a document pipeline budget is to let an LLM inspect every document before you have performed lightweight filtering. This sounds obvious, but the bottleneck is invisible at the prototype stage. A single model call is cheap, and it works well on the 20 documents in your test set.
