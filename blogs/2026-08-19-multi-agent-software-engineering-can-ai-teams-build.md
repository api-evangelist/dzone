Large language models have evolved from simple chat interfaces into autonomous systems capable of planning, reasoning, and interacting with external tools. The next stage of this evolution is multi-agent software engineering, where specialized AI agents collaborate to solve complex business workflows instead of relying on a single monolithic model. A planner may decompose work, researcher agents retrieve enterprise knowledge, coding agents generate implementations, reviewer agents validate outputs, and execution agents perform approved actions.
