What Makes a Good Build Server? In modern cloud-native application development, Continuous Integration, with automated building and testing of software on every commit, has become a standard best practice. This typically involves maintaining a farm of build nodes, which can be physical devices, virtual machines, or containers, that can be provisioned on demand and retired once build tasks are completed.
