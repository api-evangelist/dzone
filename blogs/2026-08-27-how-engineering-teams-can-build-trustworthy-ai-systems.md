In one fraud-review scenario I worked through, an AI assistant looked reliable during demos because it explained risk signals clearly and gave reviewers useful summaries. The issue appeared when the system met a legitimate high-value transaction with a new payee, an older device record, and incomplete context from the data source. The assistant did not fail loudly.
