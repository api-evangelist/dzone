Supply chains are graphs. Suppliers feed into warehouses, warehouses feed into distribution centers, and distribution centers feed into retailers. When we model them that way — as nodes and relationships rather than rows and columns — we unlock a set of tools that gives us the ability to ask questions about connectivity, paths, and the structural importance of individual nodes.
