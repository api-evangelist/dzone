Thirteen thousand internal screenshots from 343 technology companies are sitting in public GitHub repositories because a coding agent could not attach an image to a private pull request. Cybernews reported that the exposed material includes customer records, billing data, payment system screens, and unreleased product features. The agents appear to have completed the task using access they already held, but the route they took exposed data publicly.
