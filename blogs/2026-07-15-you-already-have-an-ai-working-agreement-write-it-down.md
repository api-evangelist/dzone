TL;DR: The AI Working Agreement Your team already has rules for using AI. Some live in templates, some in habits, exceptions, and one person’s memory. The AI Working Agreement puts the decisions that matter in one place: what the team delegates to AI, what stays human, what must be reviewed, what never enters a model, who owns which workflow, and how the agreement changes.
