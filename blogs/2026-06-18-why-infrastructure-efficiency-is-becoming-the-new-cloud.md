Infrastructure efficiency is rapidly becoming one of the most important factors determining profitability for cloud providers, managed service providers, and SaaS companies. For years, infrastructure growth followed a simple formula: add more servers, more storage, and more capacity whenever demand increased. That model worked when hardware prices consistently declined, and inefficiencies could be absorbed through growth.
