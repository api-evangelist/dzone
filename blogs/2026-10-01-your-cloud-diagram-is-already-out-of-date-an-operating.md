The Diagram-to-Deployment Gap Cloud security architecture often begins with a strong design, account boundaries are defined, identity federation and vending patterns are selected, centralized security services are planned, and diagrams show how telemetry, governance, and incident response should work together. The design may be reviewed by experienced architects and approved by risk stakeholders. Yet the most difficult part starts after deployment.
