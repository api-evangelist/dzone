Software engineers often feel most comfortable with hard skills, such as writing code, studying frameworks, experimenting with databases, reviewing architectures, or building side projects. As engineers advance into leadership roles, technical depth becomes even more critical. Technical leaders must guide decisions that impact other engineers, teams, and sometimes entire platforms.
