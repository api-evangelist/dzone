Software organizations require rules to ensure predictability, knowledge sharing, and sound technical decisions. However, excessive rules, processes, and standards can obstruct progress if they eclipse desired outcomes. This longstanding tension in software engineering is now more pronounced as AI accelerates code generation, solution exploration, and implementation of change.
