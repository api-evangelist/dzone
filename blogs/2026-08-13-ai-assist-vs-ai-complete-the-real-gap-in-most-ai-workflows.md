A few weeks ago, I participated in a 24-hour AI hackathon where we built a product using AI. Necessary tools were provided, a large number of engineers participated eagerly, and a few business folks also joined to bring their ideas into a real-world product using AI. During brainstorming, people drafted end-to-end process flow diagrams and started working on development, using all the recent available models and platforms to build their product.
